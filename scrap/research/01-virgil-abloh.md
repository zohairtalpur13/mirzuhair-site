# Virgil Abloh — methods and strategy

Trained in civil engineering and architecture (IIT master's). Creative director for Kanye West (Watch the Throne, Yeezus packaging), founder of Pyrex Vision and Off-White, artistic director of Louis Vuitton menswear. DJ throughout. Believed there are no borders between mediums, only human-perceived ones.

## Cheat codes (Harvard GSD lecture "Insert Complicated Title Here", 2017)
- **Readymade** (after Marcel Duchamp): start from an existing object and recontextualise it. Pyrex Vision printed on Ralph Lauren flannels.
- **3% approach**: edit a familiar object by about 3%; the kept 97% is curatorial judgement. Nike "The Ten" (2017): moved swooshes, exposed foam, printed labels "AIR" and "SHOELACES", zip ties.
- **Quotation marks**: irony and critique of systems he worked inside ("SCULPTURE", IKEA "KEEP OFF" rug).
- **Tourist vs purist**: work that satisfies the casual fan and the expert at once.
- **Design is DJing**: remix existing material; the UN logo on DJ flyers drew a cease-and-desist he framed in the retrospective "Figures of Speech".
- **Perfectionism is a trap**: work in progress; designed on his phone.
- **Trash bin theory**: only make things that add a new thought. "Nothing New" answered Raf Simons with a collection.
- **Failure is a ghost**: permission to start is yours.

## Strategy
- Text as the logo; industrial belt, diagonal stripes, zip tie.
- Collaboration as a medium: Nike, IKEA Markerad (receipt rug, "Still Loading"), Vitra, Rimowa "See Through" transparent suitcase, Evian, Mercedes.
- Invitation as object; runway set as argument (Louis Vuitton SS19 rainbow runway, Palais Royal; "False Façade" printed architecture).
- Narrative before product: Louis Vuitton FW21 built on James Baldwin's "Stranger in the Village".
- Opening the door: Free Game (free A–Z guide to building a brand), Post-Modern scholarship.
- Archive as a living work: Virgil Abloh Archive, canary---yellow, ~20,000 objects.
- Social content: Melting Passes football kits for a Paris team of West African migrants; voter-registration shirts.
- Late works: the "LIFE" plate (2021), text without quotation marks, shatter pattern.

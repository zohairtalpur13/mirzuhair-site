# Motion reference — studio.baseline "Why design trends always come back around"

Instagram post by studio.baseline (Cologne), captured by the user as IMG_2700.MOV (6 seconds).

- Base: a clean off-white card, grotesk sans headline plus large serif, small meta labels top-left.
- Crumpled paper balls fly onto the card and unfold into historic posters: an orange Swiss modernist poster, a black-and-white Nike "Future Movement" poster, a vintage New Balance advert, a "15 Minutes" sneaker ad.
- Each poster opens with its creases still visible (fold shading, grain), overlaps the others, then crumples and flies off.
- The loop returns to the clean card — the pendulum effect: trends return; skeuomorphism coming back.
- Caption theme: nothing in design is ever really gone.

## Applied to SCRAP
Base card = SCRAP masthead. Six paper balls unfold into the six legend posters (Virgil Abloh, Maison Margiela, John Galliano, Alexander McQueen, Saint Laurent, Schiaparelli), stack, crumple into the bin, masthead returns. The reveal is the table of contents.

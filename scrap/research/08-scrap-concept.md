# SCRAP — a fashion magazine made from what got thrown away

Speculative student project by Mir Zuhair (fashion, textile and communication design). Homage to six legends; not an official collaboration with any house.

## Name
SCRAP: paper in the bin, and slang for a fight. Backups: CRUMPLE, FINAL_FINAL_v2.
Tagline idea: nothing is ever really gone (from the motion reference's pendulum).

## Links to method
- Virgil Abloh "perfectionism is a trap" → every cover starts as a discarded draft.
- Readymade → the bin's contents become the luxury object.
- Material arc: paper → ceramic → gilded metal ("from the bin to the vitrine").

## SCRAP HOME (objects)
- Porcelain paper balls slip-cast from real crumpled paper, gold edges.
- Crumpled vase cast from a paper bag.
- Glazed ceramic wastepaper bin with gold-embossed SCRAP mark.
- Blind-embossed invitation on cotton paper with a real crumple texture, in a crumpled glassine envelope.
- Engraved metal contributor cards.
- Gilded brass crumpled-paper earrings and brooch (with Schiaparelli chapter).

## The Bin Room (architecture)
White gallery, floor ankle-deep in crumpled paper, finished covers hung perfectly, porcelain paper balls on plinths, a Rive Droite-style object shelf. Opening night photographed with real people.

## Realism pipeline
Scanned paper, film grain, halftone, riso misregistration, fold shadows; mockups in real places (newsstand, coffee table, metro, wet pavement); Blender renders then 1–2 physical ceramics; direct flash with film grain.

## Deliverables
Identity, animated crumple reveal, 6 covers, 6 editorials, SCRAP HOME ceramics, embossed invitation, Bin Room interior, web issue.

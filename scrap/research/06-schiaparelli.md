# Schiaparelli — codes

- Elsa Schiaparelli: Surrealism with Salvador Dalí (lobster dress, shoe hat), trompe-l'oeil knits, shocking pink, keyholes and padlocks, insect jewellery.
- Daniel Roseberry (creative director): gilded brass worn on the body as anatomy.
  - Gilded brass lungs necklace with rhinestones, Couture FW2021-22, worn by Bella Hadid at Cannes 2021.
  - Sunglasses covered in eyes; gold ears, noses, toes.
  - The viral "beating heart" necklace.
  - Idea: jewellery as an organ; metal as part of the body.
- Metal brought into fashion as an aesthetic material, not only hardware — shared ground with Virgil Abloh's brass and aluminium jewellery.

## SCRAP chapter 06 "Gilded"

Room: velvet salon, gilded plaster ears; the shoot framed through a pink keyhole.

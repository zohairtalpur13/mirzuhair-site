# Maison Margiela — codes

- Four white stitches on the back of garments; the blank label numbered 0–23 with one number circled.
- Bianchetto: white paint over objects, furniture and garments; everything painted over.
- Anonymity: veiled or covered faces; the designer never photographed.
- Replica line: faithful copies of found garments, labelled with origin and date — a readymade logic shared with Virgil Abloh.
- Tabi split-toe shoe; deconstruction and trompe-l'oeil.
- John Galliano's Artisanal SS24 show (January 2024) under Pont Alexandre III, Paris: a derelict 1920s speakeasy, half the audience at café tables under the bridge, broken plates and paper streamers, antique mirrors as video monitors. Inspired by Brassaï's night photographs of Paris and Kees van Dongen's paintings.

## SCRAP chapter 02 "White Out"
Cover: veiled face; the crumpled paper painted white so creases show only as shadow.
Object: white-glazed tableware, numbered, stamped with four stitches.
Room: an apartment painted entirely white, dust sheets, film-noir night lighting.

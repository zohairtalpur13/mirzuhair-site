Fashion magazine · Art direction · Typography · Interaction

Five houses. None of them exist.

“RAW” The Houses Issue is a black-and-red fashion magazine of five invented luxury houses. Each one is art-directed in the spirit of a real house, taking its attitude but not its name: the night-flash glamour of Saint Laurent, the deadpan of Balenciaga, the opulence of Gucci, the anonymity of Maison Margiela and the quotation marks of Virgil Abloh.

There are no products, no models and no photographs: every house is sold by typography alone, one campaign poster and one editorial page each. The issue lives on a table: drag a page and it lifts and curls like paper; move across it and a camera flash follows your hand. It is meant to be handled, not scrolled.

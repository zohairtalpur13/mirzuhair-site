Every object in the house has an issue.

The Talpur haveli in Hyderabad was built in 1843 and is still lived in. Its rooms are full of things that have outlived everyone who used them: a ruby glass vase on the dining table, a painted porcelain ewer, a jewelled yellow lantern in the hallway, a carved walnut clock, Bohemian and cranberry glass, and two tables standing on carved ivory. My textile work has always started from these objects. TALPUR gives each of them a magazine cover of its own.

Each object is modelled and rendered from my photographs of it, with its real colours, gilding and painting, and set in front of the repeat pattern I drew from the house. In most of those prints the object itself appears, so every cover shows the thing and its drawing together. The covers arrive the same way as SCRAP’s: folded, then opened flat.

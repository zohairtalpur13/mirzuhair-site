Questions

What does heritage-led mean?
Brands whose story comes from a place, a culture or a family: a chai house started from a grandmother's recipe, skincare built on ubtan, a modest-fashion label, a Pakistani kitchen in London. If your roots are part of what you sell, this studio is for you.
How do you avoid the clichés?
I research the specific craft, building or script your brand comes from and draw from that directly. No stock paisleys, generic mandalas or "exotic" fonts. It should feel as considered as a European house, with a history that is yours.
I'm not South Asian. Can we work together?
Yes. Global brands can bring me in for South Asian collections, campaigns and launches when they want the work to be authentic rather than borrowed.
Does distance matter?
No. We work over video calls, email and shared folders, and I keep hours that overlap with Europe, the Gulf and North America.
Do I own the designs?
Yes. Full rights to the final identity transfer to you once the project is paid in full.

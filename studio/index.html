Questions

Before you ask.

What do you mean by "heritage-led"?

Brands whose story comes from a place, a culture or a family: a chai house started from a grandmother's recipe, skincare built on ubtan, a modest-fashion label, a Pakistani restaurant in London. If your roots are part of what you sell, this studio is for you.

How do you avoid the clichés?

No stock paisleys, generic mandalas or "exotic" fonts. I research the specific craft, architecture or script your brand comes from and draw from that directly, the way my own work comes from the Talpur havelis of Sindh. The result should feel as considered as a European luxury house, with a history that is yours.

I'm not South Asian. Can I still work with you?

Yes. Global brands can bring me in for South Asian collections, campaigns and market launches when they want the work to be authentic rather than borrowed. Any brand built on a real heritage is welcome too.

I'm in the UK, US, Canada or the Gulf. Does distance matter?

No. Everything happens over video calls, email and shared folders, and I keep overlapping hours with Europe, the Gulf and North America. Invoices are in USD, GBP or EUR.

Do I own the final designs?

Yes. Full rights to your final logo, identity and assets transfer to you once the project is paid in full.

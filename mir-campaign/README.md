# MIR — What remains.

A complete first digital campaign study, with editable artwork and clearly identified concept mockups.

## Open

Open index.html for the portfolio presentation. Motion and the interactive piece are linked inside. For reliable canvas image/video export, serve this folder locally:

    python3 -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/. No packages, accounts or network assets are needed.

## Files

- index.html / style.css: full campaign presentation.
- CASE-STUDY.md: extended brief, reasoning, actual revisions, problem solving and reflection.
- motion.html / motion.js: playable twelve-second film, timeline, frame PNG and browser video export.
- interactive.html / interactive.js: postcard maker, three source motifs, persistence and scale controls, keyboard/touch input, PNG export.
- artwork/: MIR vector signature, card front and reverse, three posters, scent strip, textile label.
- assets/: original source drawings and doorway photograph, plus two application mockups.
- VALIDATION.md: checks completed and known production limitations.

## Production status

The SVGs are editable artwork masters. Artwork containing type uses system fonts; outline or package approved fonts before printer delivery. They do not include printer-specific bleed, crop marks, color separations or CMYK conversion. Mockups are material concepts and may differ in fine detail from the master artwork. No packaging dieline, manufactured product or physical print test is claimed.

## Mockup art direction

Stationery: MIR vermilion and ivory business cards, the supplied original doorway illustration used across the reverse and correspondence; blind-embossed black envelope, small M wax seal, tactile cotton paper and raking light. Preserve the architectural character of the reference. Brand MIR only. No invented contacts. Original product-mockup photograph, landscape 3:2.

Packaging: preserve the existing MIR green glass bottle and oxblood cap; use the supplied doorway drawing on an ivory carton and vermilion side panel; include a narrow MIR scent strip. Warm ivory wall, red plinth, strong sunlight, realistic glass and paper. Brand MIR only. Landscape 3:2.

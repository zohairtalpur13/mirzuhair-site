Typography · Motion · Interaction

Text and textile share one root.
In Latin, texere means to weave.

Move to comb the threads · click to pluck

02

Unravel

Every letter is a row of threads that agreed to stay. Scroll, and they let go one by one. Scroll back, and they knit themselves together again.

03

Weave

Type a word and the loom weaves it. Where a letter is, the weft rises over the warp; everywhere else a twill holds the cloth together. The same over and under that makes every fabric.

04

Tension

A thread only shows its strength when it is pulled. Grab a letter and swing it. Double-click to cut a thread.

05

Repeat

My prints are built by mirroring one drawing until it becomes cloth. Here your word does the same. Move across it to change the scale and the weight; click to change the dye.

Every textile
is a text.

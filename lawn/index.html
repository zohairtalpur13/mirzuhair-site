The suitRs 3,990

The hands that picked its cottonRs 80

Every summer, Pakistan buys lawn by the season: printed, embroidered, unstitched, three pieces in a box. I grew up in Sindh, where much of that cotton is grown and picked by hand, mostly by women. I wanted to know how much of a suit's price reaches the field. Some of the answer is public. Most of it is not, and that is part of the answer.

02

Walk it back to the field

واپس کھیت تکWapas khet tak · back to the field

Cloth is made from yarn, yarn from fibre, fibre from the raw cotton a woman pulls from the boll. Each step loses weight, so the field has to give more than the shop receives.

  1. Shop

    0.72 kg

    of printed lawn, folded into a box.

  2. Mill

    0.88 kg

    of cotton fibre, before about 18% is lost to spinning, weaving and finishing [E2].

  3. Gin

    2.68 kg

    of phutti (پھٹی), raw seed cotton. In Pakistan only about a third of it is fibre; the rest is seed and trash [3].

  4. Sack

    A maund (من) is 40 kg, the sack pickers are paid by. One suit fills 6.7% of it.

  5. Field

    About 37 minutes of one woman's picking, out of an eight-hour day [4].

06

Method and sources

طریقہ اور حوالےTareeqa aur hawale · method and references

How each number above was worked out, so you can check it or change it.

  1. Cloth 5.75 m × 1.4 m × 90 g/m² = 0.72 kg
  2. Fibre cloth ÷ (1 − 0.18 loss) = 0.88 kg
  3. Raw cotton fibre ÷ 0.33 lint yield = 2.68 kg
  4. Picker raw cotton × (rate ÷ 40 kg) = Rs 80
  5. Fibre value fibre × (Rs 18,400 ÷ 40 kg) = Rs 406
  6. Tax price × 18 ÷ 118
  7. Minutes raw cotton ÷ 35 kg a day × 480 minutes
  1. [1] Gul Ahmed, Lawn 2025, 3-piece printed lawn suit CLP-52230: dupatta 2.25 m, shirt 1.75 m, dyed trouser 1.75 m, listed at Rs 3,890.
  2. [2] Lawn and voile fabrics typically weigh 60 to 100 g/m²; fabric weight guides, 2025.
  3. [3] Business Recorder, Weekly Cotton Review, 21 Sep 2026: KCA spot rate Rs 18,400 per maund; phutti in Sindh Rs 8,000 to 8,600 per 40 kg. Lint is about 33% of raw cotton in Pakistan (Business Recorder, “More seed cotton or more lint?”).
  4. [4] D+C Development and Cooperation: women pick 30 to 40 kg of phutti to earn a day's wage. IJWHR: pickers work about eight hours a day.
  5. [5] Regular 2026 prices for unstitched three-piece lawn suits at a national retailer: printed Rs 3,990, embroidered Rs 7,250, luxury embroidered Rs 19,990.
  6. [6] Arab News, 2024: women cotton pickers in Shaheed Benazirabad, Sindh refused Rs 800 per 40 kg and won Rs 1,200 through their union.
  7. [7] Pakistan federal budget 2024–25: Tier-1 textile and leather retailers charge the standard 18% sales tax.
  8. [8] Government of Sindh, minimum wage notification 2025–26: Rs 40,000 a month, Rs 1,538 a day for unskilled workers.
  9. [9] Clean Clothes Campaign: estimated living wage in Pakistan Rs 47,627 (US$286) a month; surveyed garment workers earned 74% less.
  10. [E1] Estimate. A shirt length of 1.75 m only makes a kameez if the cloth is wide, so I assumed 1.4 m.
  11. [E2] Estimate. Combined losses in spinning, weaving and finishing of roughly 18%.

This is a model, not an audit. Estimates are marked E. Change one and the picker's share moves by a few rupees; the shape of the answer does not.
